# Recipe: Travel & Trip Planning

Use this recipe when users plan a vacation, weekend getaway, offsite, or multi-day trip.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Multi-Day Trip Itinerary**:
   - Synthesizes destination, travel dates, highlights, and budget per traveler.
   - Schedules the trip into Google Calendar with pre-filled details or direct API.
2. **Flight Recommendations**:
   - Recommends realistic flight routes with airline names (e.g. Singapore Airlines, ANA, Scoot, Cathay Pacific), departure/arrival times, estimated price ranges, and booking links.
3. **Hotel Recommendations**:
   - Curates top-rated accommodations based on group size, neighborhood, star rating, price per night, and amenities (e.g., near transit, breakfast, quiet).
4. **Travel Notifications & Alerts**:
   - Sends alerts for flight check-in reminders, baggage guidelines, passport/visa requirements, and destination weather.
5. **Card Tools**:
   - `travel_plan_card`: Trip title, destination, dates, travelers, highlights, calendar button, maps link.
   - `flight_hotel_card`: Curated flight options & hotel stays.
   - `travel_alert_card`: Travel notifications (check-in, weather, packing, reminders).
